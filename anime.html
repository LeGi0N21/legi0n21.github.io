Story of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

Part 1 - Phantom Blood

Phantom Blood follows Jonathan Joestar, a noble and kind-hearted young man whose life is turned upside down when his adopted brother, Dio Brando, begins plotting to steal the Joestar family fortune and inheritance. After discovering an ancient stone mask that transforms Dio into a powerful vampire, Jonathan must master an ancient martial art called Hamon to fight back against Dio’s growing supernatural army. As their rivalry escalates, Jonathan embarks on a dangerous journey to stop Dio and protect the people he loves, leading to an epic clash between justice and pure evil.

Part 2 - Battle Tendency

Battle Tendency follows Joseph Joestar, the brash and clever grandson of Jonathan Joestar, who discovers that an ancient race of powerful beings called the Pillar Men has awakened. These mysterious beings created the Stone Mask and seek a powerful artifact that will allow them to become virtually invincible. With humanity's survival at stake, Joseph must master Hamon and use his wit, trickery, and unconventional fighting style to defeat enemies far stronger than himself. Alongside allies like Caesar Zeppeli and Lisa Lisa, Joseph faces increasingly dangerous battles in a globe-spanning adventure that tests his courage and ingenuity.

Part 3 - Stardust Crusaders

Stardust Crusaders follows Jotaro Kujo, a tough and rebellious Japanese high school student who discovers he has a supernatural ability called a Stand—a manifestation of one's fighting spirit. When his mother, Holly, falls dangerously ill due to the awakening of her own Stand, Jotaro learns that the villainous Dio Brando has returned, now wielding a powerful Stand of his own. With Holly's life at stake, Jotaro joins his grandfather Joseph Joestar and a group of allies on a journey from Japan to Egypt to defeat Dio before it's too late. Along the way, the group battles a series of Stand users, each with unique and deadly abilities, in a race against time to save Holly and end Dio's reign of terror.

Part 4 - Diamond Is Unbreakable

Diamond Is Unbreakable follows Josuke Higashikata, the illegitimate son of Joseph Joestar, who lives in the seemingly peaceful Japanese town of Morioh. When Josuke discovers his Stand ability, Crazy Diamond, he becomes involved in a series of strange supernatural incidents alongside his friends. However, the town's bizarre events conceal a much darker secret: a serial killer named Yoshikage Kira is living among the townspeople, using his deadly Stand, Killer Queen, to hide his crimes. As Josuke and his allies investigate, they must uncover Kira's identity and stop him before more innocent people become victims.

Part 5 - Golden Wind

Golden Wind follows Giorno Giovanna, the son of Dio Brando who carries the Joestar bloodline, as he dreams of becoming a “Gang-Star” and taking control of Italy’s criminal underworld to stop the drug trade that harms innocent people. After joining the powerful mafia organization Passione, Giorno teams up with a group of Stand users, including Bruno Bucciarati, to overthrow the mysterious boss who rules the organization. However, their mission quickly becomes a deadly struggle involving betrayal, powerful enemies, and the secrets behind the boss’s identity. As Giorno and his allies fight their way through Italy, they must decide how far they are willing to go to achieve justice in a world ruled by crime.

Part 6 - Stone Ocean

Stone Ocean follows Jolyne Cujoh, the daughter of Jotaro Kujo, who is framed for a crime and sentenced to prison in Florida. While incarcerated, she awakens her Stand ability, Stone Free, which allows her to unravel her body into strings. When Jolyne discovers that her imprisonment is part of a larger plot orchestrated by a mysterious priest named Enrico Pucci, she must fight to protect her father and uncover a conspiracy connected to the legacy of Dio Brando. With the help of fellow prisoners and their unique Stand abilities, Jolyne battles to stop Pucci from carrying out his plan to reshape humanity's destiny.

Part 7 - Steel Ball Run

Steel Ball Run takes place in an alternate universe in 1890s America, where a massive cross-country horse race offers a prize of $50 million to its winner. The story follows Johnny Joestar, a former jockey who became paraplegic, and Gyro Zeppeli, a mysterious competitor who uses a technique called the Spin. The two form an unlikely partnership as they race across the country, encountering dangerous rivals and uncovering a conspiracy involving the race's true purpose. As the competition progresses, Johnny discovers new powers and becomes entangled in a struggle over a collection of mysterious body parts with extraordinary abilities, drawing him into conflict with powerful figures who have their own ambitions for the nation.

Part 8 - JoJolion

JoJolion takes place in the same alternate universe as Steel Ball Run, primarily in the Japanese town of Morioh following a devastating earthquake. The story follows a mysterious young man discovered buried beneath the rubble, with no memories of his identity or past. Given the name Josuke Higashikata, he begins investigating his origins with the help of Yasuho Hirose, gradually uncovering strange phenomena involving mysterious objects called the Locacaca, which can exchange one thing for another through a process known as equivalent exchange. As Josuke uncovers the secrets of the Higashikata family and Morioh itself, he becomes entangled in a bizarre mystery involving supernatural abilities, hidden identities, and a dangerous group seeking the power of the Locacaca.

Part 9 - The JoJoLands

The JOJOLands takes place in modern-day Hawaii and follows Jodio Joestar, a teenage delinquent who aims to become extremely wealthy in a world driven by money and material success. Living with his brother Dragona and working for a local criminal organization, Jodio gets involved in a mission to steal a valuable diamond from a wealthy Japanese tourist. However, the heist leads him and his companions to discover mysterious objects with supernatural properties, setting them on a dangerous path toward acquiring immense wealth. As Jodio and his crew navigate Hawaii's criminal underworld, they encounter bizarre Stand abilities, dangerous rivals, and mysteries surrounding a strange volcanic phenomenon known as lava rocks, which appear to attract valuable things.
