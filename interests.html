My Hobbies/Interests

American Football

I got into this sport back in 2013. My friend Jacob suggested I watch an NFL game as he was really into the sport at the time. Seattle Seahawks were defending Super Bowl champions for the year and were playing in the quarter final match against the Caroline Panthers. I watched a player in the 'tight end' position catch the ball and refused to be taken down by 3 other players and fell in love with the sport. I played the sport in Melbourne for 2 years also. It's the only game I can watch and enjoy watching it

Warhammer 40k mini painting

I have recently got into the hobby of miniature painting. My group of friends are into painting warhammer and through total peer pressure but I have not regreted it at all. My friend group meet up once a week for a few hours and just hang and paint and it's an awesome time. I am currently painting the space marine chapter called "Black Templars" (Pictured) then I was paint the chaos space marine chapter called "Black Legion"

Gaming

I have been gaming for as long as I can remember. I have such fond memories of playing Conkers Bad Fur Day on the nintendo 64, playing mario kart, mario party and guitar hero with my sister on the gamecube and nintendo wii. My favourite game of all time is probably Saints Row 2 as the memories and friends I have made from that game has impacted my life so much that I don't think I would be the same person I am today without it. If I could go back and re-live one day back then I would give up anything.
